A full-stack developer in Denver, Colorado who turns business needs into working software.

My development journey started with teaching myself HTML, CSS, and JavaScript basics. That initial spark of interest grew into a passion that led me to Denver University's Full Stack bootcamp, where I got to plan, build, and deploy real-world projects alongside other developers.

### 🚀 Some things I've built

**[Almadelic](https://www.almadelic.com/): patient acquisition & checkout**<br/>
I built the front door of our patient journey, from the marketing site and blog CMS to the CRM integration that carries a lead through purchase to first treatment. When our WordPress storefront hit its limits, I volunteered to replace it with a custom React and Stripe checkout on AWS Lambda. It shipped in about a month, cut payment fees from roughly 6% to 3.5%, and let the company start generating revenue.
<br/><sub>React · Stripe · AWS Lambda · API Gateway · CloudFront · GoHighLevel</sub>

**[AlmaEHR](https://site.almaehr.com/): a custom EHR**<br/>
Our third-party EHR was getting expensive and couldn't support our specialized treatment sessions, so we went looking for alternatives and ended up building our own on Google Cloud's Healthcare API, using FHIR R4 so it plays well with other systems. Costs dropped by roughly 75%, and clinicians got the tools they needed to run day-to-day operations.
<br/><sub>Next.js · Node/Express · FHIR R4 · Cloud Run · Cloud SQL · BigQuery</sub>

**[Retail site](https://gardensdispensary.com/): multi-location rebuild**<br/>
We rebuilt a retail site spanning 17 locations across five states in under two months, with SEO, accessibility, and AI crawlability built in from the start. Paired with the marketing team's local SEO work, several stores now rank in the top 1–3 local search spots in Denver.
<br/><sub>Next.js · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS</sub>
